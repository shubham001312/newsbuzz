About NewsBuzz

NewsBuzz is a digital-first news platform dedicated to delivering fast, accurate, and trustworthy news from West Bengal and across India.

Our Mission

We believe every reader in West Bengal — from Kolkata to Cooch Behar, from Darjeeling to Diamond Harbour — deserves timely access to news that matters to them. NewsBuzz was founded to fill that gap with honest, no-frills journalism in both English and Bengali.

What We Cover

Our editorial team covers breaking news across eight key areas: West Bengal local news, national India news, government initiatives and schemes, politics, crime & law, education, sports, and weather. We pride ourselves on being among the first to report on stories that affect everyday life in Bengal.

Our Standards

NewsBuzz is committed to factual, balanced reporting. We follow PTI/ANI editorial standards for wire-service reporting. We correct our errors promptly and transparently. We do not publish unverified rumours or sensationalised content.

Languages

All our articles are available in English. Select articles are also available in Bengali (বাংলা) to ensure maximum accessibility for our readers across West Bengal and the Bengali-speaking diaspora.

Advertising

NewsBuzz is monetised through display advertising via Mediavine. We do not accept paid editorial content or sponsored articles that are presented as news. All advertising is clearly labelled.
